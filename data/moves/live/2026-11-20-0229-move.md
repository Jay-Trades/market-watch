## 3. Theories
### H1: Technical break of Thursday's low, with stops and long liquidations in thin Asian hours (low-medium)
- FACT: The previous low was 61,381 at 21:32 UTC (TradingView via Finwire). The alert print, 61,370, is just below it. 24h liquidations were $528M, about 80% longs (CoinGlass, via Finwire/CryptoPotato).
- FACT: CryptoQuant puts a cost-basis concentration at $62,000–$65,000 (via Invezz, 11-19), and Invezz's chart levels are $61,300, then $60,100, then the $57,803 cycle low. The CryptoQuant zone has a named measurer. The 61,300/60,100 levels are chart technicals, not positioning data.
- INFERENCE: the hour's slide into and just through a widely watched low, with ZEC (a higher-beta altcoin) falling twice as much, looks like leveraged longs being flushed. That is how a break in thin liquidity usually plays out.
- Against it: we have no liquidation data for **this hour**; the $528M figure covers the prior 24h. An hour of −1.44% that ends only $11 below the prior low isn't a clear cascade yet. If BTC reclaims 62,000 quickly, it was noise.

### H2: Macro squeeze carrying into Asia: dollar, December Fed hike bets, metals collapse (medium as background, low as this hour's trigger)
- FACT: Gold fell −5.45% on Thursday (JSON, z −3.99). Alert headlines at 00:34 and 01:51 UTC: dollar set for a fourth weekly gain on Fed hike bets, gold and silver "plunge further." FxPro (CoinDesk, 11-19) cited Fed minutes showing openness to hikes larger than 25bp. 10Y 5.05%.
- INFERENCE: a rising dollar and real yields are pulling money out of every non-yielding asset at once, gold and BTC alike. The 01:51 gold headline suggests metals were still falling during the hour.
- Against it: we can't verify that the dollar, yields or gold actually moved during 01:29–02:29 (degraded data). The headlines describe the trend, not a new event. A slow macro grind doesn't usually produce a 3.5x one-hour move by itself.
