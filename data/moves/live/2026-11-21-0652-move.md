## 3. Theories
### H1: Short covering after a liquidation flush, in thin weekend liquidity (low-medium)
- FACT: CoinGlass shows about $2.4B in crypto derivatives liquidated over 24h, more than $1B of it BTC futures, with the largest single wipe-out a $51.7M BTC long (via Investing.com, 05:56 UTC on Nov 21).
- FACT: Wintermute said recent rebounds "reflected short covering rather than new buying demand" (via Investing.com). Snippets report perpetual funding slightly negative near the lows (−0.0058% and −0.0023% in two reports). SECONDHAND: we found no exchange or source for those funding figures, so they don't count as evidence.
- INFERENCE: once leveraged longs have been cleared out, forced selling dries up. Funding turning negative means shorts are paying to stay in, which sets up a sharp move when price ticks up. At 2am ET on a Saturday, with no stock market, a modest wave of buying or short covering can move BTC 3–4% in an hour. That fits the "7.9x normal" size even without a catalyst.
- Against it: we have no liquidation breakdown for 06:18–06:52 UTC, so we can't see shorts actually getting forced out. A CryptoSlate claim of "$12.7B short squeeze risk" above price has no clear date, so it's SECONDHAND and unused. Also, the 4h window is still negative, so this hour may just be noise inside a downtrend.

### H2: Buyers stepping in at a key price zone (low)
- FACT: Analysts cited by The Currency Analytics (Nov 21: Wintermute, Rekt Capital, Benjamin Cowen) point to $50–54k as the key support zone, with "realized price just under $54,000." The low is also BTC's lowest level since September 2024 (Investing.com).
- INFERENCE: a ~10% fall in 24h down to a two-year low may have triggered resting bids and spot dip buying.
- Against it: SECONDHAND. The realized-price figure is attributed to the article, and we couldn't find the underlying Glassnode data. The bounce started around 55.4k, above that zone, and nobody reported a clear spot buyer. There's also no weekend ETF flow to point to.

### H3: A news catalyst (ZEC merger approval, anything else) (low; not supported)
- FACT: Fortitude, the DCG-backed Zcash miner, won shareholder approval for its HeartSciences merger and a Nasdaq listing as TUDE (headline 04:59 UTC).
- Against it: ZEC fell from about 711 to 693 in the hour after that headline. It rose only once BTC rose, and by roughly 1.8x BTC's move, which is ordinary high-beta behavior. When the deal was announced in June, ZEC fell ~7% that day (The Block). The other headlines in the window (the Binance-hack laundering story, Treasury pressure over Iran sanctions) are bearish or unrelated. **No clear catalyst.**

What we can't see: hourly liquidations by side, open interest changes, exchange-specific funding, Coinbase premium, order book depth, and any large single-wallet or OTC trade. Friday's ETF flows publish Monday (Farside). None of this is guessed.
