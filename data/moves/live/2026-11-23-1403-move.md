## 3. Theories
### H1: The Nvidia reinstatement set off a cross-asset relief rally (medium)
- FACT: Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate Huang as president, CEO and director, effective immediately. It said it took input from senior US government officials and found no national-security risk, and that the investigation by its independent committee will continue (Nvidia press release / 8-K, Nov 23).
- FACT: CoinDesk's 09:03 ET entry ties NVDA +27%, Nasdaq futures +6% and BTC +4% to $63k together. Thin premarket prints in the JSON show SMH +16.2% and QQQ +6.9%, with VIX −12%.
- INFERENCE: Friday's selloff ran from AI and Nvidia into crypto (KuCoin: "deleveraging in AI trades spread to… crypto"). Undoing the Nvidia leadership shock undoes some of that same trade. Crypto, which was the most oversold (RSI 20), shorts-heavy and illiquid in thin premarket hours, moved the most.
- Against it: BTC's peak (08:55 ET) came before the only verified timestamp of the news (08:57:54 ET EDGAR). We can't see whether the hour's climb started at 08:05 or at 08:50. The previous Nvidia-driven BTC pop, after earnings on Nov 17–18, faded within about 35 minutes (Cointelegraph/CryptoSlate snippets). ZEC has no direct link to Nvidia.

### H2: A follow-on short squeeze in an extremely oversold market (medium-low as a cause, likely as an amplifier)
- FACT: Over the weekend, shorts dominated liquidations on the rebound from ~55.1k (CoinGlass via crypto.news, prior report). Funding was briefly negative and Binance OI is down ~40% y/y (KuCoin, which doesn't cite a provider). Fear & Greed is at 11 (The Crypto Desk, snippet).
- INFERENCE: once BTC broke through the 62–63k resistance band named in KuCoin's daily, short covering would speed the move. That would explain why it is 8x normal rather than simply following equities. ZEC's +8% (high beta, −53% off high) fits a squeeze better than any news about the coin itself.
- Against it: we found no CoinGlass liquidation figure for this hour, so it can't be confirmed. Decrypt's "$63K reclaim liquidates $540M" is from a different date.

### H3: Strategy's buy or Bessent's Fed comments (low)
- FACT: Strategy bought only 457 BTC ($29.6M) for the week, which is small next to its $211.8M of preferred buybacks. It was filed at about 08:12 ET, inside the window (CoinDesk).
- FACT: Forbes (08:35 ET) reports Bessent warning the Fed against hikes. The 10Y is +0.08% and the 3M yield +0.51%, so rates show no dovish repricing (JSON).
- INFERENCE: neither is big enough or confirmed by the tape. The Binance-hack laundering story (Elliptic, 08:54 ET) is bearish or irrelevant, and the Nvidia SEC-probe headline (07:23 ET) points the wrong way.

What we can't see: 5-minute BTC bars inside the hour, CoinGlass liquidations and funding for this morning, Coinbase premium, and Friday's ETF flows (latest found: Nov 17, −$239.9M per Farside via Cointelegraph). We didn't guess any of these.

## 6. Warning signs

- Pointed the wrong way: the Nvidia SEC-probe headline (07:23 ET) and the Binance-hack laundering story, both bearish, came just before the jump.
