## 3. Theories
### H1: Nvidia's governance crisis escalates into an accounting question (medium-high for semis and the last two hours; medium for the index)
- FACT: Reuters, citing two people briefed, says SEC enforcement staff are examining potential accounting issues at Nvidia. Reuters couldn't determine the exact focus or start date. The SEC declined to comment (via Decrypt and Korea Times, Nov 20). The WSJ reported a probe on Tue 11-17 but didn't name its subject.
- FACT: Jensen Huang resigned as CEO last week after reports of an affair with Alice Huang, formerly of Megaspeed, a Singapore Nvidia customer that U.S. authorities have probed since 2024. The board rejected his offer to return on Tuesday. The FT reported this week that Nvidia stopped breaking out Singapore revenue as it approached nearly half of sales. Reuters didn't say whether that change is part of the probe (Decrypt). Today: "Former Nvidia CEO Huang agreed to testify at public US Senate hearing" (Reuters, via Investing.com, ~1h before 20:56 UTC) and "Trump... urges Nvidia's Huang to apologize."
- INFERENCE: "accounting" turns a governance story into a question about how good the earnings are. Nvidia's weight, and its role as the AI bellwether, pulled down SMH, AMD and AVGO. It is also why a forward P/E of 7.5 gives no support (§4).
- Against it: SPY and BTC were already sharply lower before ~14:17 ET. MU (−14.65%) and AMD (−14.12%) fell as much as NVDA without an SEC story of their own. A probe was already known since Tuesday, so only the "accounting" detail is new.

### H2: Broad AI-trade liquidation and cross-asset deleveraging (medium)
- FACT: This is the seventh down session out of eight for SPY, which was −5.45% over 5d and −8.4% off its high before today (JSON). Anthropic (ANT) shares are giving back most of their debut premium toward the $83 IPO price (Investing.com). Investing.com: "Nasdaq's weakness prompts investors to shift from tech to bonds." Equal weight is −2.2% vs SPY −4.0%, and staples are up.
- FACT: Gold −4.63% and silver −7.88% while the 10Y fell 14bp and the dollar was flat (JSON and Investing.com ticker).
- INFERENCE: when gold, silver and crypto sell off together as yields *fall*, that usually means selling to raise cash or meet margin, not repricing to higher rates. This is a different picture from Thursday's hike-driven move. A crowded AI book unwinding into a correction fits the steady, headline-less slide from 09:55 ET.
- Against it: we have no positioning data (prime-broker de-grossing, CTA triggers, margin calls). "Deleveraging" is inferred from cross-asset co-movement, not measured. High yield (HYG −0.31%) is calm, which argues against a credit-driven forced sale.

### H3: Crypto's own deleveraging, with equities as the accelerant (medium for BTC; low on cause for ZEC)
- FACT: U.S. spot BTC ETFs saw −$582.3M on Thu 11-19, the third straight outflow day, about −$3.47B month to date (Farside, via World Today News: secondhand, low-quality outlet). 24h crypto liquidations were about $1.5B (CoinGlass via CoinDesk, cited by The Crypto Basic). Fear & Greed was 12 (CoinGecko via Coingabbar, 01:30 UTC). BTC's 14:15 ET low came before the Nvidia headline, and its last hour was only −0.5%.
- INFERENCE: BTC is trading as a high-beta risk asset in a deleveraging tape. It broke its Asian-session low (previous report), then followed stocks lower through midday. ZEC (−17.6%, with −9.4% already done overnight) is the high-beta altcoin version. No ZEC-specific news was found.
- Against it: the liquidation figures conflict (U.Today says $391M vs $1.5B), and the ETF figure is secondhand. No ZEC catalyst was ruled out, only not found. A Japan/carry-unwind story (Coingabbar) has no dated yen or JGB move behind it here.

What we can't see: today's ETF flows (published after the close), funding rates and open interest, prime-broker de-grossing and CTA flows, single-stock options and gamma positioning, and NVDA block trades. None is guessed.
