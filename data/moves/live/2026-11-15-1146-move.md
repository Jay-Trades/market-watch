## 3. Theories
### H1: Liquidation exhaustion and oversold relief (medium for the overall bounce, low for the 4h leg)
- FACT: About $2.02B was liquidated crypto-wide in 24h. BTC liquidations were ~$750M, of which ~$600M were longs (CoinGlass via BigGo/TokenPost). Longs outnumbered shorts in liquidations by roughly 4–10x, depending on the snapshot.
- FACT: Coinbase volume peaked in the low hour (~3,400 BTC). Cryptoticker's own read: "the overnight recovery is mainly the end of a liquidation wave."
- FACT (JSON): RSI14 was 22.5 before today, 5d −14.75%. FACT: Fear & Greed was 10 Sunday, 13 Sat, 20 Fri (Alternative.me via Cointelegraph).
- INFERENCE: once leveraged longs are gone, sell pressure dries up and a modest bid lifts price in thin weekend books. The low-then-retest-then-higher-high pattern (62.0k → 65.6k → ~63.8k → 65.9k) fits a market finding its footing.
- Against it: this explains the 01:40–03:00 UTC bounce better than the 07:46–09:10 UTC leg, which came hours after the liquidation peak. The 1h stall and the 24h window still negative (−2.6%) mean this is a bounce inside a downtrend, not a reversal. The cause (ETF outflows, Fed hike pricing) hasn't changed.

### H2: Short covering and positioning before the weekly close and CME open (low)
- FACT: CME's Friday close was ~$68,200, leaving a gap of ~$3,400 (~5%) at current prices (Cryptoticker). CME reopens 23:00 UTC today, and the weekly candle closes at 00:00 UTC.
- FACT: BigGo cites a weekly close above $67,850 (roughly the 200-week MA, see the 2108 report) as the bull-case trigger.
- INFERENCE: shorts who sold the $65k break are taking profits, and some traders are buying to avoid a weekly close at the lows. Gap-fill trades often target levels like the CME close.
- Against it: Binance funding was still **positive** (+0.0043%/8h) at 07:04 UTC, just before the leg. Shorts weren't crowded enough to pay to hold. No short-OI or short-liquidation figure for the window was found. Untestable as stated.

### H3: A news catalyst (no clear catalyst)
- FACT: No dated story for 07:46–09:10 UTC turned up in searches for Bitcoin, Zcash, Saylor/Strategy, or Sunday crypto news. Saylor's Sunday tracker posts (Nov 1, Nov 8) came before Monday buys, but none was found for today, and they usually land later in the US day.
- FACT: Both alert headlines are bearish or irrelevant, and the ETH piece comes after the high.
- INFERENCE: ZEC's +11.6% since Saturday 4pm ET (vs BTC +1.1%) is high-beta rebound behavior, not a ZEC-specific event. No dated ZEC news was found for 11-15.
- Verdict: no clear catalyst.

What we can't see: liquidations, funding and OI for 07:46–09:10 UTC; Coinbase premium (US vs offshore buying); exchange inflows/outflows; options skew. Equities, yields and DXY are frozen at Friday.

- Only obvious in hindsight: the retest near ~$63.8k held well above the $62.0k low (a higher low) before the 07:46 leg.
- Pointed the wrong way: Fear & Greed fell to 10 Sunday morning, and the ETH-selloff headline came out while price was already holding. Funding still positive argued against a squeeze.
- Could it have been predicted? A bounce after a $2B flush, yes, in general. The timing of this specific 4h leg, no. With no catalyst, it could only be traded as a reaction to a higher low.
