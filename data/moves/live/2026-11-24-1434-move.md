## 3. Theories
### H1: The Nvidia relief rally unwinds, and crypto falls with the AI trade (medium)
- FACT: CoinDesk (05:08 ET) says BTC "hands back Monday's NVDA-driven rally" as "the surge in AI stocks… began to unwind". Reuters (08:38 ET) has NVDA −5.7%, Micron/AMD/Marvell −5.6 to −7%, and CoreWeave, IREN and Nebius −5%.
- FACT: SMH is −5.6% and the worst sector, and QQQ −1.9% vs SPY −1.2% (JSON).
- INFERENCE: the same cross-asset trade that lifted crypto on Monday is reversing today, and crypto, being the highest-beta, gives back more.
- Against it: the timing is backwards. BTC and ZEC did most of their falling on Monday afternoon while NVDA held +31%, and BTC's low (05:45 ET) came before the equity lows (08:10–08:30). Crypto led the reversal rather than following chips. FalconX's Lim (via CoinDesk): NVDA recovered over 80% of its post-ouster losses but BTC only ~25%, so the two were never fully linked.

### H2: No spot demand. Record ETF outflows and Fed-hike and dollar pressure (medium)
- FACT: Monday's spot ETF flow was −$569.7M even on a +4% BTC day. November is −$4.71B, a record (Farside via CoinDesk). A December 25bp hike is "all but certain" (CME FedWatch via Reuters). PCE is due Wednesday.
- INFERENCE: Monday's jump was short covering into ETF selling. With no buyer underneath, the price drifted back to where it started, which is what the prior report warned of. This explains why crypto faded *before* chips did.
- Against it: these pressures are not new and don't explain a sudden 24h drop. Yields are flat and the dollar is −0.5% today (JSON), so there's no fresh macro shock this morning.

### H3: ZEC-specific: governance overhang plus high beta (low)
- FACT: A coinholder vote on pulling 50,000 ZEC (~55%) out of the Zcash Community Grants treasury and ending ZCG's 8% issuance share runs Nov 16–30. It is non-binding (CryptoTicker Nov 16; CoinDesk Nov 12). ZEC was −3.3% to ~752 by 06:31 ET (CoinDesk).
- INFERENCE: the dispute weighs on ZEC holders, but the vote is old news. ZEC's −18% vs BTC's −5% looks like its usual 3x beta (it was +8% vs BTC's +3.7% on Monday).
- Against it: we found no ZEC news dated today. CryptoTicker itself says there's no evidence the vote caused earlier drops.

Not drivers: the Binance-hack piece (Bloomberg 13:00 UTC) is a background story on a hack dated Oct 29–30. The Ansem wallet hack ($2M of memecoins) is too small. The dormant-BTC/lawsuit story (12:47 UTC) came after BTC's low and has no price effect we can see. Scaramucci and the Brookline kiosk ban are irrelevant.

What we can't see: CoinGlass liquidations and funding for the last hour, Coinbase premium today, MSTR/COIN premarket, Tuesday's ETF flows (out tonight), and any ZEC-specific order flow. CoinDesk says funding is "barely positive" and BTC OI −1.0% vs price −3.0%, which reads as longs reducing positions rather than a fresh short pile-on.
