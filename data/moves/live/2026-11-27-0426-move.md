## 3. Theories
### H1: Oversold bounce, leveraged buying off the 690 floor (medium for the mechanics, low for a cause)
- FACT: CoinGlass ZEC futures OI ~2.16M ZEC Friday vs 1.97M the day before (+~10%), with ~$122M of futures inflows in 24h, "far above spot" (CoinGlass via FXStreet, Nov 27 04:04, snippet). The rebound started from the $690 floor after a $682 close, the lowest daily close in three months (FXStreet).
- FACT: Before Wednesday, ZEC had RSI14 24.7, was −17.4% over 5 days and −56% off its high (JSON).
- INFERENCE: A deeply oversold coin at a round-number floor, plus new long positions, gives a sharp mechanical snapback. It matches the +9% in a few hours with a flat last hour.
- Against it: the OI number is FXStreet's reading of CoinGlass. We couldn't see the CoinGlass page or a liquidation heatmap ourselves, so we can't tell whether this was new longs or short covering. "Oversold" is not a catalyst, and ZEC has been oversold for a week.

### H2: Broad crypto risk-on into the weekend (low to medium)
- FACT: BTC is +1.55% over 4h. HYPE is +4.82% (CoinMarketCap headline, 03:04). CoinDesk (Thu) says DeFi and infrastructure tokens were gaining about twice as much as BTC on its bounce from 57.5k. Binance's withdrawal reopening is complete (Nov 26–27 coverage).
- INFERENCE: A firmer BTC lifts high-beta altcoins, and ZEC is among the highest-beta.
- Against it: ZEC's move is about 6x BTC's, double its usual ratio. The Binance story is a day old. The Nvidia export-curb headline (04:01) is risk-off and came after the bounce, so it doesn't explain anything.

### H3: "Last privacy coin standing" after KuCoin's Monero delisting (low)
- FACT: KuCoin is delisting four XMR spot pairs on Nov 30 08:00 GMT, citing only "platform policies". ZEC pairs remain on KuCoin with no notice (Startup Fortune, citing Crypto Economy). Bifu reports ZEC +2% and DASH +8% while XMR fell.
- INFERENCE: Privacy-coin traders could rotate from XMR to ZEC, which has optional transparency and is still listed widely.
- Against it: ZEC *fell* ~11% on the same day as the delisting news, and several outlets asked "Is Zcash next?" (BeInCrypto, Crypto Economy), which is a bearish framing. Today's bounce comes ~12h+ after the notice, with no new delisting news in the window.

Not drivers: the Kangwon Land buyback, the Embraer tariff and the Nvidia export curbs (04:01, after the move and risk-off) are unrelated or mistimed.

What we can't see: hourly ZEC bars inside the window, CoinGlass ZEC liquidations and funding, ZEC spot volume by venue, Grayscale ZEC trust flows, and ZCG vote tallies so far.

## 5. Contrarian view
- Bullish case: ZEC is −56% off its high with RSI in the mid-20s, sitting on a support zone at 690–720 that analysts flagged a week earlier (Coinpedia, Nov 22). If KuCoin and other venues keep ZEC while dropping XMR, it becomes the default listed privacy coin (INFERENCE from Startup Fortune and Bifu).
- Bearish case against the bounce: FXStreet (Nov 27): "highly leveraged positional buildup backing a near-term recovery trend amid the broader market's downside pressure fizzles out, trapping buyers in a long squeeze." Coinpedia (Nov 22) saw the broader trend as still bearish after the drop from 1,600–1,700.
- SENTIMENT: we found no dated Reddit or X posts for this window. Earlier 2026 coverage cites Lyn Alden calling the autumn run a "coordinated token pump", which is old context, not today's crowd.
- For the contrarians (bulls) to be right: ZEC holds above 720 through Friday's US session while OI flattens, meaning spot buyers take over from futures.

## 6. Warning signs
- Visible beforehand: (1) Extreme oversold readings (RSI14 24.7, −34% vs 50d MA) in the Nov 25 JSON. (2) Support at 690–720 named by Coinpedia on Nov 22. Both set up a bounce but gave no timing.
- Only obvious in hindsight: that the 690 floor would hold at 00:00–00:30 UTC Friday rather than break.
- Pointed the wrong way: the KuCoin XMR delisting and the "Is Zcash next?" coverage (Nov 26) were bearish for privacy coins, and ZEC dropped about 11% that day.
- Could this have been predicted? Only as "an oversold bounce is likely somewhere near 690". The timing and size, no.

## 7. What to watch next
1. **ZEC 700 / 690 (support) and 770–800 (Wednesday's close, Nov 22 resistance).** A loss of 700 with OI still high means a long squeeze is under way (FXStreet's trap). A close above 770 erases Thursday's drop.
2. **BTC's Friday options expiry (~$7B) and the US half-day.** A weak BTC after expiry would test whether ZEC's bounce has any support of its own.
3. **Privacy-coin delisting news through Nov 30, and the ZCG vote closing Nov 30 23:59 UTC.** A ZEC notice from any major venue would undo the bounce. A clear vote result removes an overhang.
