## 3. Theories
### H1: Mechanical break of $65k into thin weekend books (medium for this hour's leg)
- FACT: CoinGlass liquidation maps (via TokenPost/ChainCatcher, published before today) showed about $485M of cumulative long-liquidation intensity on major exchanges below $65,000. That is a relative intensity measure, not a dollar forecast.
- FACT: Coinpedia (11-13) and Bitcoin.com (11-14) both flagged $65k/$64k as support, with $64.5–64.0k as the next target if it broke. The 78.6% Fibonacci level sits at $64k. Levels this widely watched are where stops cluster.
- FACT: On Saturday morning funding was still positive and longs still outnumbered shorts (TokenPost). Leverage hadn't been flushed out by then.
- INFERENCE: the slow drift in the 4h window, followed by a sharp hour once $65.0k gave way, looks like stops being run. A move this fast with no news is what a stop run looks like. ZEC's −3.3% hour at roughly 2x BTC's move fits beta selling across crypto, not anything specific to ZEC.
- Against it: I couldn't find a liquidation total for 20:00–21:00 UTC, so the cascade is inferred, not measured. Liquidation maps are estimates, and the $485M was measured at a different price.

### H2: The weeklong macro and flow drain is still running (medium for the trend, low as the trigger for this hour)
- FACT: Dec FOMC hike odds are ~88–89% for 25bp across CME, Polymarket and Kalshi, up from 76% on Nov 6 (Bitcoin.com). Bitcoin.com also cites Oct headline CPI +0.4% and core +0.3%, Brent above $100 and Oct payrolls of +165k against ~65k expected. **Single source**: the CPI detail matches Axios, but I didn't verify Brent or payrolls.
- FACT: Spot ETFs had 7 straight outflow days through Friday (−$185.4M Fri, Farside/SoSoValue). Glassnode shows stablecoin supply down $14.2B over 30 days. Both are in the 17:37 report.
- SECONDHAND: "whales sold 34,000 BTC (~$2.6B) in a week." Ali Martinez said it (via KuCoin, Bitcoin.com, ZyCrypto). It refers to roughly Nov 7–13, and I didn't find the underlying on-chain dashboard. A falling wallet balance isn't proof of selling. Not used as a cause.
- INFERENCE: this explains why buyers aren't stepping in at support. It doesn't explain why the selling sped up at about 15:00 ET on a Saturday.
- Against it: nothing new arrived Saturday afternoon. FedWatch odds were already priced by 13:16 ET, and ETFs are closed.

### H3: Selling ahead of a weak weekly close (low)
- INFERENCE: BTC is set to post its first weekly close below the 200-week MA (~$67.8k per Cryptoticker) of this cycle. Traders may be de-risking before Sunday's weekly close and Monday's ETF and equity open, especially after the equity drawdown (QQQ −1.2%, SMH −3% Fri; SMH −11% on Nov 5).
- Against it: this can't be tested. No flow data separates de-risking from forced selling, and the weekly close is still ~27h away.

What we can't see: liquidations, funding and OI for the last hour; Coinbase premium (US vs offshore selling); exchange inflows; options skew. Equities, rates and DXY are frozen at Friday levels.
