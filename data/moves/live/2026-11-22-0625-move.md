## 3. Theories
### H1: A base effect: the window starts at the low, and the squeeze is already over (medium-high)
- FACT: the alert's implied 24h-ago price (~56,010) sits right on Saturday's 06:18 UTC low area (prior report). The 4h window is −1.43%, and the JSON shows BTC −2.41% from Saturday 4pm ET with the overnight high at 16:15 ET.
- FACT: CoinGlass, as cited by crypto.news on Nov 21, shows shorts dominating liquidations during the rebound ($81.3M shorts vs $3.2M longs over 4h). Bitcoin.com News puts 24h liquidations at $2.67B (CoinGlass), mostly longs, from the crash leg.
- INFERENCE: the 24h number is mechanically large because yesterday's capitulation low is about to fall out of the window. Within the next hour or so, the 24h change should shrink toward roughly +3–5% even if price doesn't move.
- Against it: a base effect doesn't explain why the squeeze carried as far as ~60.5k (+9.8%). That part needs H2. ZEC is also up 1.9% in the last hour, which is a small live move of its own.

### H2: A short squeeze plus some dip buying at the lows (medium; this explains Saturday's rally, not this hour)
- FACT: QCP Capital: "short-covering rallies may struggle to develop into sustained upside." Glassnode, via crypto.news, says buyers absorbed ~66,000 BTC in the $62–66k zone. CryptoQuant's Darkfost says a record ~$950B of invested capital is sitting at an unrealized loss (Bitcoin.com News).
- INFERENCE: with funding negative and a crowded short side after Friday's crash, a small bid at 55k forced shorts to cover. Once they were cleared out, the push faded, which fits the lower highs since Saturday 16:15 ET.
- Against it: CoinCodex shows 24h volume down 34% (06:00 UTC). Without volume we can't see spot demand. No weekend ETF flows exist, so dip buying can't be confirmed.

### H3: A news catalyst (low; not supported)
- FACT: the only headline in the window, D'CENT shutting its App Wallet after a hack, concerns a wallet drained mostly of XRP in September (Crypto Briefing, Cointribune). It is bearish or irrelevant for BTC, and BTC was near its overnight low around that time. CoinCodex lists no headline that explains the move. **No clear catalyst.**

What we can't see: liquidations by side for the last 12 hours, open interest, exchange-specific funding, Coinbase premium, order book depth. Friday's full ETF flow figure isn't available until Monday. None of this is guessed.
