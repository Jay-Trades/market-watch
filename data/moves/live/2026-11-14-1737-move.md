## 3. Theories
### H1: Fed-hike repricing and a strong dollar (medium)
- FACT: October core CPI was +0.3% m/m against 0.2% expected, the third straight 0.3% (Axios, 11-10, snippet). Waller set 0.3% as the bar for considering a 50bp December hike (Stocktwits, snippet).
- FACT: Markets price ~64bp of hikes over 12 months, up from 37bp earlier in November (Coinpedia citing Coinbase Institutional weekly, 11-13). Coinbase Institutional named Fed expectations, not the Binance hack, as the main driver.
- FACT: The 10y closed Friday at 5.15% and DXY sits at a 19-month high (TokenPost, 11-14).
- INFERENCE: higher real rates and a stronger dollar push down a non-yielding, liquidity-driven asset. That fits the trend since Oct 21.
- Against it: none of this is new on Saturday. CPI came out four days ago. Friday's PPI was *softer* than expected, and BTC still sold off after it. Macro explains the trend, not the timing of today's leg.

### H2: Flow drain from ETFs, stablecoins and the Strategy/MSCI overhang (medium)
- FACT: Spot BTC ETFs saw −$185.4M Friday and seven straight sessions of outflows. The week's total was −$1.07B, after −$1.28B the week before (Farside, via Trending Topics). SoSoValue gives the same Friday figure (Investing.com). IBIT has had 11 straight outflow days, about −$2.35B (Farside, via Trending Topics).
- FACT: Stablecoin supply fell $14.2B in the 30 days to Nov 9, the fastest drop since June 2022 (Glassnode, via Decrypt/CryptoSlate, snippets). Coinbase Institutional separately measured −$11.5B over 30 days (Coinpedia).
- FACT: MSCI will drop MSTR from its USA Index after Nov 30 (The Block). JPMorgan's ~$2.8B passive-selling estimate shows up in Coinpedia and Bitcoin Magazine. I couldn't find the underlying JPM note: SECONDHAND.
- INFERENCE: the marginal buyer (ETFs, stablecoin dry powder, Strategy's capital-raising) has turned into a seller, so bids are thin.
- Against it: ETFs don't trade on Saturday, so they can't explain today's tape directly. MSTR selling hits the stock, not BTC; Strategy isn't forced to sell coins.

### H3: Weekend liquidity and a long-liquidation cascade (medium-low, as the cause of *today's* leg)
- FACT: CoinGlass shows about $1.0–1.47B in 24h liquidations, 82–93% of it longs. Totals differ by snapshot time: Investing.com ~15:14 GMT said $1.47B; Trending Topics 17:00 GMT said $1.01B. About $553M hit in the four hours to ~17:00 GMT.
- FACT: Price broke Friday's ~$67.5k low overnight (TokenPost). Fear & Greed is at 13, down from 20 Friday (Trending Topics).
- INFERENCE: breaking a well-watched low in thin Saturday books likely tripped stops and liquidations. That would explain the sharper overnight leg without any news. The 1h bounce from the 12:05 low fits a flush that has run its course.
- Against it: liquidations amplify a move but don't start one. And I couldn't verify a liquidation heatmap showing clustered levels near $66–67k.

### H4: Anthropic IPO pulling liquidity (low)
- FACT: Anthropic's IPO seeks up to $64.4B. Pricing is expected Nov 18, unconfirmed (TNW, FourWeekMBA, snippets). Strive's Matt Cole compared BTC's slide to the one before SpaceX's IPO (Investing.com).
- Against it: this is a narrative with no flow data behind it. Nothing ties the BTC sellers to IPO subscriptions.

What we can't see: live funding rates and open interest (no dated Nov-14 CoinGlass/Coinalyze reading was found), Coinbase premium, exchange inflows and options skew. ETF flows resume Monday. Rates, DXY and equities are frozen at Friday levels.

## 6. Warning signs
- Visible beforehand: Glassnode's stablecoin contraction (−$14.2B through Nov 9, the fastest since June 2022) was published around Nov 11. Coinbase Institutional's 11-13 weekly flagged the $66–68k band with $62k below it. Waller said before CPI that 50bp should be considered if core came in at 0.3%. Every one of these is dated before today.
- Only obvious in hindsight: Friday's failed bounce above $70k after a *soft* PPI. Buyers wouldn't hold good news, and that only looks telling now.
- Pointed the wrong way: Friday's softer PPI (0.3% vs 0.4%). RSI was already below 30 days ago, which looked like a "bounce soon" setup.
- Could it have been predicted? The direction, yes: falling flows and hike repricing were documented all week. The timing and size of a Saturday leg, no. Tradeable only as trend-following, not as a call on the event.
